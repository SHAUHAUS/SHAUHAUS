DISPATCHES: CROSS-DISCIPLINARY COLLABORATIVE CREATIVE COMMUNICATION


BCH 4503/6503 SCIENTIFIC COMMUNICATION SKILLS


ART 3313 GRAPHIC DESIGN I


This project is a collaboration among the students of ART 3313 Graphic Design I and BCH 4503/6503 Scientific Communication Skills. The task: To effectively communicate scientific research and design to the public via the creation of scientific dispatches.


A DISPATCH is a condensed (and accurate) representation of a scientific paper, often used to highlight and promote key points of research.


30 scientific research topics have been synthesized and visualized across 30 small groups. Each group worked collaboratively, communicating across their disciplines in order to ensure clear, appropriate, and effective communication of the research and execution of the design outputs.


BCH 4503/6503 Task: Synthesize a scientific paper into a 750 – 1000 word dispatch (highlight).


ART 3313 Task: Visualize a scientific dispatch into a well designed editorial format (article).
